Questions, answered

Is it free?

Yes — download and duel for free. We sell optional story campaigns and character skins. Nothing you can buy affects a fight.

What platforms?

iPhone, iPad, and Android phones and tablets.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Practice against Fable, play story campaigns, and solve the daily puzzle without a connection. Online duels need one, naturally.

How long is a match?

Around five minutes. Long enough for a plan, short enough for a queue.

Do I have to know tabletop RPGs?

Not at all. If you can plan two moves ahead, you can play. The dice add drama, not homework.
